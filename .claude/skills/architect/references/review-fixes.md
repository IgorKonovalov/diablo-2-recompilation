# Common review fixes — architect (the reviewer's blind spots)

What later reviews or lanes had to correct in the architect's own plans and reviews. Check a plan or a
review against these before committing it. Categories are those of the [lane run ledger](../../../../docs/plans/metrics.md).

1. **Recount the items a phase lists** when drafting it. Seen: plan 0039 Phase 1 said 16 for P1a's 15
   and 20 for P1b's 18 ([0039](../../../../docs/plans/done/0039-backlog-sweep-4.md) P1a and P1b reviews).
2. **A claim settled at review gets the lane's scrutiny.** "Open wounds halve by `0x0C` only" was settled
   at the resume-lane review; the run could not tell it from "`0x0C` or `boss`" (Andariel carries both),
   and the `lane/p37t` review had to scope it ([0037](../../../../docs/plans/done/0037-combat-formulas.md)). The `lane/h1a` review settled stat 72's 8-bit width "for an item version ≤ 0x5F"; the test reads `ITEMS_ReadFull`'s version argument, the save's (the `v94` case's items kept item version 101), fixed at the `lane/s38s` review ([0038](../../../../docs/plans/done/0038-inferred-claims-sweep.md)). The `lane/h6b` review labelled "the 253 half" of sock 18 d-none without saying it was the schedule, not the call its ledger raises; `lane/s38k` kept the call `[I]` (fixed at the `lane/s38k` review, 0038). The `lane/p54c` review ordered Phase 3 to state the live grid `[C]` over "20 671 + 13 893", a count holding the 842 plans its own ruling had taken out of stock; the spec writes 33 722 (fixed at the `lane/s54` review, [0054](../../../../docs/plans/done/0054-path-search.md)). The ruling relayed to `lane/p55c` that sub-missiles step "at their own sites" was never read against `SUnitCreate`, which steps every unit at `UnitSeedInit`+0x41; the reader's even rule and control 13 were built on it (`lane/p55c` review, [0055](../../../../docs/plans/done/0055-server-frame.md)).
3. **Write a review fix into the document it changes**, not only into the review's fix list. Seen: S4a's
   preset 18 split, still "raised" in drlg/0004 at the `lane/p38s3` review ([0038](../../../../docs/plans/done/0038-inferred-claims-sweep.md)); the `lane/s38h2b` review's items/affixes count (I 28) missing from its research-map row, fixed at the `lane/s38h2c` review (0038); the `lane/h4a` review marking class 312's H4b piece met in monsters/0004 but not special 66's in monsters/0003, fixed at the `lane/p51r` review ([0051](../../../../docs/plans/done/0051-backlog-sweep-6.md)). The H4a raise of the wanderer list written into monsters/spawn only, lifecycle's Constants row left `[I]` until the `lane/sfx` review (0038). The `lane/h1b` review accepted "S1a pieces met (the rows stay S1a)" for two pieces its own kept check compared (rows 24 and group 52); a met piece with a compared line moves at that review, fixed at the `lane/s38s` review (0038). The `lane/h6gs` audit routed the grant's cut `T(L + 1) − T(L)` to player/creation and wiki/characters but not to quests/progression's word-39 bullets, which kept "between the current and the next level" and the altar's "instead" (fixed at the `lane/s38p` review, 0038).
4. **Read the whole branch region before ruling.** Seen: the Phase 2 review missed the moving player's
   `÷ 3`, found at Phase 3 (0037). The `lane/p50d` review passed anim/0003 § 6.5's `HasCollision0` anchor for event 1's
   `+0x122` (`HasCollision2`) test, fixed at the `lane/a3f` review ([0050](../../../../docs/plans/done/0050-animation-frame-timing.md)). The `lane/h6e2` review passed the open question's "`OBJS` lists only the objects near the player" without reading `tools/gdb/vendors.py`'s walk, which filters neither class nor distance (corrected at the `lane/rdp` review, [0038](../../../../docs/plans/done/0038-inferred-claims-sweep.md)).
5. **Recompute totals once, over every reviewed batch, after merging `main`.** Two reviews computed in
   parallel each missed the other (S2b and S3a, fixed at the S4b review, 0038).
6. **After every lane merge, run `python3 -I tools/backlog.py archive`** (ADR-0019; it runs `migrate` first, then
   sweeps closed entries into `docs/backlog/archive/` and fixes links; on a conflict in `docs/backlog.md` keep both
   sides, migrate dedupes and keeps the strike). Before ADR-0018: dedupe by entry text, keep the struck copy. Seen: the S2b, S4b,
   H4b, `lane/p38s3` and S1e merges (0038).
7. **Label a plan's estimates as estimates.** "No logger change" in a batch recipe was a guess (H5a ruling
   4, 0038).
8. **Give every reviewed spec a wiki owner and every plan its research-map rows at the start.** Seen:
   "wiki: not part of this plan" (0037 P3 → `lane/wiki1` ruling, 0038); the stats rows due when plan 0041
   started ([0041](../../../../docs/plans/done/0041-stats-and-states.md) P1 review); the `skills/dispatch`, `skills/missiles` and
   `skills/catalogue` rows, due when plan 0045 started, added only at the `lane/p46m15` review
   ([0046](../../../../docs/plans/done/0046-skills-function-catalogue.md)); and keep a row's status current at each phase acceptance: the
   `skills/dispatch` and `skills/missiles` rows still read "Phase 2 and the spec not started" after the part 1 review
   (fixed at the `lane/p45k` review, [0045](../../../../docs/plans/done/0045-skills-missiles-machinery.md)). The `items/economy` row's counts left at `I 83 / L 12` while the front matter had moved to 84 / 12 (fixed at the `lane/s45p3` review, 0045). The `anim/timing` row still reading "P1.2 next" after the P1.2 review, and `anim/movement` "not started" (fixed at the `lane/p50d` review, [0050](../../../../docs/plans/done/0050-animation-frame-timing.md)). Inventory § 5's batch table left without "(done)" for H1b, H3b, H4a, H5c, H5d and H7a after their reviews (fixed at the `lane/h1a` review, [0038](../../../../docs/plans/done/0038-inferred-claims-sweep.md)). The objects row still reading "the Khalim heart chest (gold first; eye and brain `[I]`)" after the `lane/h6e2f` review accepted all three (fixed at the `lane/s38q` review, 0038).
9. **A departure a record states is still a ruling to make.** Test it against the ADR's words, not against how harmless it looks. Seen: an `IsBadCodePtr` import slot given a game function, stated in run:2026-10-30-frame-emu-3 and passed at the `lane/r55c` review with no ruling on ADR-0015 (1); found at the `lane/r60c` review, ADR-0029 ([0060](../../../../docs/plans/done/0060-item-use-inventory.md)).
10. **A cross-check compares against the whole reviewed record.** The Phase 1b entry told the lane "against path/0001 to path/0006", and five verdicts were `not covered` for that reason alone. Name the specs and tables the findings rest on. And a lane's "nothing to compare" is a claim: the `lane/p54c` review ruled "no registered source" true of `docs/sources.md` without grepping the rows or opening `src:d2dfg-blizzard`, and it was false (`lane/r1b` review, [0054](../../../../docs/plans/done/0054-path-search.md)). The `lane/p60b` check and the `lane/s60` review passed "what decides it is not known" for a potion's list, with stats/states E2 and E4 in the spec's own `depends_on` (`lane/r60d` review, [0060](../../../../docs/plans/done/0060-item-use-inventory.md)). A named claim with no source is not a verdict: it goes outside *Leads used*, with the record's answer.
11. **A scan a review or an ADR cites gets the lane's own rule: kept, recounted, over the set the rule names.** Seen: ADR-0029's "25 lock calls" from a range scan at the `lane/r60c` review, which missed two `call edi` and 3 of the 8 sites the cases reach; corrected by `lane/r29` ([0060](../../../../docs/plans/done/0060-item-use-inventory.md)).
12. **A word for a table is a claim about its values: recompute it over the table.** A model or an emulation that reads the table can't test the word, and a control that tests a bound doesn't either. Seen: `g_PathSignTable`, "the signs of dx and dy", passed at the `lane/r54a` and `lane/s54` reviews with the reads beside it, 81 of 81 another rule, found by `lane/a54`; the `i8` of a 64-`i32` table, caught at the `lane/s60` review; `g_PathStep8Long` and `g_T15Step` as plain `i32` where the readers load words, `lane/a54` ([0054](../../../../docs/plans/done/0054-path-search.md)).
13. **ADR-0031 binds what a review writes too: try a rule before writing a constant, in an entry or a log as much as in a doc.** Seen: the `lane/a54` review's entry on `g_PathFaceStep` gave its 64 values by run and asked for them in a spec, and the `lane/a54b` review's search for a dump read the ruled tables only, so the sentence reached `main` (c763dc6f); an exact rule gives the table, found at the `lane/s70` review ([0054](../../../../docs/plans/done/0054-path-search.md)).
14. **A list a ruling gives is checked against the ADR it rules under.** The P1 review's four shared seeds left out what ADR-0022's context names, "a defender's seed hit twice in one frame"; the reader was built on the list and its first pack window stopped the lane (`lane/p55b` review, [0055](../../../../docs/plans/done/0055-server-frame.md)). And a reader's coverage of a plan's question is part of a tools check: the `lane/p55a` check passed a window reader that tested the order only on shared-seed pairs.
15. **A register a review traces to its store has to be traced to its last write, and that may sit inside an inline seed step.** The `lane/s52b` review read row 18's `push ebx` as `s.unit`; `xor ebx, ebx` at 0x005F2403, for the step's `adc`, had zeroed it. Its own scan of the 51 sites was right that nothing else was non-zero, and the review's correction made it false. `lane/s52c` then repeated the reading on rows 49 and 74 from a collapsed listing; found at the `lane/s52c` review ([0052](../../../../docs/plans/done/0052-monster-ai.md)).
16. **A limit a finding states is a claim: read it against the log before ordering a tag on it.** The `lane/p55c` orders and the `lane/s55` review passed "the logger does not log the unit" for the defender and owner rows, and the brief for `lane/p55d` expected whose seed at `[I]`; the logger doesn't log the target, but an `XS` line logs the low word, and a kept seed names itself. Found at the `lane/p55d` review, with the 8 steps on the player's seed ([0055](../../../../docs/plans/done/0055-server-frame.md)).
