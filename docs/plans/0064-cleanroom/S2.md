  printed diffs (row 2). The fitted magic find is the only reason the final score is clean. Without it, 136 cases (quality and
  everything after it on the seed) stay wrong.

  and chain worked example 3 (pick, select, subtc, quality, count, end seed), and the NoDrop values 10 and 20 of items/tc
  worked example 3.
