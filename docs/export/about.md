# About

This is an unofficial, non-commercial preservation project. It describes how Diablo II: Lord of Destruction 1.14d
works, so that its behaviour is not lost and can be rebuilt faithfully.

## What it is not

- **Not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.** Diablo and Blizzard Entertainment are trademarks of
  Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. The game's names are used only to say which game is described.
- **Not a copy of the game.** No game files, art, text dumps or code are included. Descriptions of behaviour are
  written in our own words. To play the game, you need your own copy.

## A simplified edition

This site is a shorter edition of the project's notes. Where a sentence, list item, table row or section could not be
published as written, it was left out rather than reworded. A page may therefore skip a detail, or mention a
specification that is not part of this site. The shortening only removes: it never rewords or adds to what the notes
say about the game.

## Diagrams and animations

The diagrams are drawn from text when the site is built. The animations are schematic drawings of our own: a level's
rooms appear as labelled boxes with their doors and their entry and exit marks, and a dropped item appears as a name
card coloured by its quality. They hold no pixel of the game's art and use our own colours. Values shown in them are
computed by our own model of the rules on the page, and the seeds they start from are illustrative.

Diagrams and animations illustrate the text. When they differ, the text is what counts.
