## Mismatches

- **Main prediction: 86 predictions. At the first check 6 failed (the sweep predictions below); after the checker's change 0 mismatches, 6 excluded on an independent record.** The committed file predicted the 6 player drops made in the Act IV town (frames 100..1000, `+0x24` 15 100..16 000) freed at the sweep of frame 16 500. They were not: the character had left the town, its rooms were inactive, and no later sweep dump lists them. The prediction did not condition on the room staying active; this is the harness's error, not the game's. The checker was changed after that first check. At the architect review (`lane/p60`) the exclusion was tightened: an item is excluded only when no later dump lists it, no free is logged, and the player's level (`PLR` `lid`, logged) differs from the drop's level (103) before every later sweep; absence from the dumps alone makes a mismatch. The 6 are excluded on that record; the room's own activity is not logged (`[I]` as the reason). The committed predictions are unchanged; the output of the tightened checker is `check-main-r2.txt` (`manifest-inventory-r2.txt`), and the first check's count (6 mismatches) is in `experiments.md`. A drop that survived in an active room would be listed in a dump and stay a mismatch, so a real negative is still caught; a drop that left the dumps by a path not logged (a pickup) while the player was elsewhere would be excluded, and none was. The 4 drops made in the active room are the sweep cases that decide.
