  (rows 217–220, the scrolls and books of identify and town portal), do 151 `0x005CA260` (row 53 Chain Lightning's
  `ItemEffect`); each `(ECX game, EDX unit; skill id, level)` with `ret 8`. The callers emulated: `ITEMSKILL_Cast`
  `0x005FDA80` (`ret 0x28`) with its helpers `0x005FD850`, `0x005FD9C0`, `0x005FDA10`, `sub_005FDCA0_ItemCastAtUnit`
  and `sub_005FDD60_ItemCastAtXY`, the stat-event handlers 20 `0x005C0470`, 21 `0x005C05A0`, 30 `0x005C0670`, the
  shrine functions 19 `0x00582DA0`, 21 `0x005830E0`, 22 `0x00583410`, and `SKILLS_PayCost` `0x0056BFE0`. All four
  entries and the ten callers (the three helpers run inside the cast) are emulated: five families, **12 000 cases, 0 mismatches**.
  (data:g_SkillStartFuncs@0x00732140, data:g_SkillDoFuncs@0x007322B0, data:g_StatEventFuncs@0x007325B0,
  data:g_ShrineEffects@0x006E1850, `static.txt`, run:2026-10-21-cat-emu-item)
- [C] **Draws before the boundaries.** Handler 20 (with no packet or packet `+0x00` bit 5) and handler 30 (always)
  step **the handler's unit's seed** once inline and compare `low mod 100` with the chance stat; handler 21 (with
  packet `+0x04` bit 2) draws `RNG_RangeB(unit+0x20, 100)`. `ITEMSKILL_Cast`'s `ItemTarget` 2 helper steps **the
  seed of the cast's caster** twice before its first collision test. Shrines 21 and 22 draw `RNG_RangeB(shrine+0x20,
  Arg1 − Arg0)` inline on **the shrine object's seed** (no step for a range ≤ 0). No other function of the batch steps
  a seed before its boundary or return. (run:2026-10-21-cat-emu-item, fn:sub_005C0470_StatEv20SkillOnEvent@0x005C0470,
  fn:sub_005FD850_ItemTargetRandomPoint@0x005FD850, fn:SHRINE_Exploding@0x005830E0)
- [C] (ADR-0021; § 3) **Unsummon.** Start 3 returns 1 and writes the target's guid to the used node's `+0x18` when the
  target is a monster (type 1) whose `MonsterOwner` is the caster, the caster a player, the target's pet type (the
  caster's pet list that holds its guid, `0x00574A20`) has PetType's `unsummon` bit (`+0x04` bit 3), and there is a
  used node; else 0. Do 4 ends at `PetRemove(ECX game, EDX caster; node+0x18, 1)` (`0x005750E0`), the 1 being the
  `push 1` left on the stack for it. (run:2026-10-21-cat-emu-item)
- [C] (ADR-0021; § 4) **Do 113** walks the caster's inventory (`+0x60`, magic `0x01020304`, `+0x0C` the first item,
  ItemData `+0x64` the next) and takes the first item that is of item type 18 (`book`) with Books `BookSkill` the skill
  and stat 70 above 0, or (not a book) of type 22 (`scro`) with `ScrollSkill` the skill and **no quantity test**; by
  its ItemData `+0x69` byte it ends at `0x0055E170(ECX game, EDX caster; guid, x, y, &arg1)` (1) or `0x00562390(…, 0)`
  (2); any other value returns 0 without looking further. **Do 151** sets bit 6, then ends at
  `SkillMissileCreate(ECX game, EDX srvmissilea; caster, id, lvl, dx, dy, 0, 0, 0)` with `(dx, dy)` the target's
  position minus the caster's (0 without a target). (run:2026-10-21-cat-emu-item)
- [C] (ADR-0021; § 5) **`ITEMSKILL_Cast`.** Refuses id 0 and `ItemEffect` 0 (no write); aims the caster's path at
  the given unit or point; writes the out type 6 and guid −1 and the out point (0, 0); by the low byte of `ItemTarget`
  (`+0x120`): 1 aims at the caster (out type and guid the caster's); **2 gives `0x005FD850` the caster** (`EDX`
  = the cast's caster, the call at `0x005FDB9F`), which ends at `COLL_PatternTest(room, x + low₁ mod 40 − 20, y +
  low₂ mod 40 − 20, path+0x48, path+0x4C)` after two steps of the caster's seed, or with no room refuses, and the cast
  then restores the target, the point and bit 6; 3 ends at `0x0056E390(ECX caster, EDX target after the aim; id,
  lvl)`; 4 keeps a target, else aims at `0x00553010`'s stored unit (`+0xC8` bit 17, `+0x9C` type, `+0xA0` guid) or none
  and writes the out type and guid (6 and −1 for none). It ends at `SKILLS_SrvStart(id, lvl, 1)` when
  `ItemCheckStart` (`+0x08` bit 1), else at `SKILLS_SrvDo(id, lvl, 0, 1, effect)`. **Helpers 3 and 4 make no draw**
  before their boundaries, and their static closures hold none (586 and 554 functions; 56 and 54 indirect calls not
  followed). (run:2026-10-21-cat-emu-item, `cg.txt`)
- [C] (ADR-0021; § 6) **The handlers.** The chance is `UnitGetStat(unit, stat, layer)` of the record's `stat << 16 |
  layer`; the skill is `layer >> DataTables+0xC6C`, the level `layer & DataTables+0xC70`. Handler 20 casts at the
  other unit (`ItemCastAtUnit`, effect 1) or at the unit's path point (`ItemCastAtXY`, effect 1); with `ItemTgtDo`
  (`+0x07` bit 5) **the other unit is the caster** and the target (effect 0), and no other unit returns 1 with no
  cast. Handlers 21 and 30 cast with effect 0, at the other unit or at the point. (run:2026-10-21-cat-emu-item)
- [C] (ADR-0021; § 7) **Charges.** `SKILLS_PayCost` returns 1 for a non-player; for a player whose used node has the
  paid id and an owner item (`node+0x34` ≠ −1) it ends at `UseCharge(EAX guid, ECX game, EDX node; caster)`; else
  the cost `(mana + lvlmana · max(lvl − 1, 0)) << (manashift & 31)`, floored by `minmana << 8` (signed), goes to
  `BloodManaPay` under state 114, or to `UnitAddStat(caster, 8, −cost, 0)` when stat 8 ≥ cost; less mana, a bad id or
  `mana` and `lvlmana` both 0 → 0. (run:2026-10-21-cat-emu-item)
- [C] (ADR-0021; § 8) **The shrine missiles.** Shrine 19 (Storm) ends at the unit search
  `0x0065A950(pool, &iter, room, x, y, Arg1, 0x00582710, &{0x583, …, +0x0C x, +0x10 y, +0x14 Arg1})` round the
  shrine; shrines 21 (Exploding) and 22 (Poison) draw the count `n = Arg0 + RNG_RangeB(shrine, Arg1 − Arg0)` and end at
  `ItemIndexByCode('opm ' / 'gpm ')` for `n > 0`, else at the first of six `MissileCreate` blocks (45 `explosivepotion`
  / 48 `chokinggaspoition`, flags `0x520`, level `clamp(clvl / 5, 1, 8)`). (run:2026-10-21-cat-emu-item)
- [I] **The data joins and the carried items.** The item effect flag is 1 only from handler 20 without `ItemTgtDo`,

### 10. Pets when `petmax` falls

- [I] **`sub_00575900_PetLimitRefresh(ECX game, EDX player)`**: for a player with PlayerData `+0x44` pet lists and a
  skill list, a frame array of 256 maxima zeroed; for each of the player's skills (`0x00643910`, `0x006438F0`) whose row
  has a `pettype` above 0 and below the count: `max(SKILLS_EvalCalc(player, petmax, id, SKILLS_GetSkillLevel(player,
  node, 1)), 1)`; **when it is above the type's running maximum**, store it and `PetLimit(game, player; type, it)`;
  then for each type whose maximum stayed ≤ 0, `PetLimit(type, PetType basemax)` (`+0x0A`, s16). (0x00575900–0x00575A7B)
- [I] **Who runs it.** `STATE_DefaultRemoveCB` calls it for a player (`0x0056E960`) after clearing the state and
  refreshing the passives (not for a dead unit whose state stays on death, which returns first), so any timed state that ends with the default callback recomputes the limits; the skill
  shrine's state uses its own remove callback `0x00583BD0`, which runs `STATE_DefaultRemoveCB` and then `0x0056DE40`
  (passives and `0x00575900` again), and `SHRINE_Skill` runs `0x0056DE40` when it applies the state. Its other
  callers: `0x0056DE70` (after `SKILLS_AddSkill`), `sub_0056DEB0_UnitSkillAdd`, `0x0056DF3F`, `0x0057013B`,
  `0x00575C63`, `0x00575C80` (8 sites, one of them `0x0056DE40`'s tail jump). So when the skill shrine wears off, the
  maxima fall with the skill levels and `PetLimit` removes the pets above them, the first of each list first, never of
  type 7 (skills/0003 § 7). (0x0056E955–0x0056E960, 0x00583BD0–0x00583BE1, 0x00583C4B–0x00583C5A, `static.txt`)
- [I] **`sub_0056BD90_PetLimitForSkill(ECX game, EDX player; id)`**, called twice by `sub_0055B800_MonStatCallback`
